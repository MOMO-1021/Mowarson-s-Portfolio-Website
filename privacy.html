Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Mowarson Developer (mowarsondeveloper.netlify.app)

At Mowarson Developer, operated by Mowarson, we prioritize the privacy and security of our clients, website visitors, and partners. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information collected, how it is processed and protected, and your rights concerning your data.

Key Summary: We only collect necessary project and communication information. We never sell or rent your personal information. All online payments are securely processed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant, 256-bit encrypted gateway.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you when you submit inquiry forms, subscribe to our newsletter, request a consultation, or complete an online payment:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number.
  • Business & Project Details: Company or business name, website URLs, project requirements, and service preferences.
  • Communication Records: Messages, feedback, and project correspondence shared via contact forms, email, or WhatsApp.

2. Payment Information & Security

All online financial transactions conducted on this website are routed securely through Razorpay Payment Gateway (Razorpay Software Private Limited).

  • Mowarson Developer does not collect, store, or view sensitive payment card details, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, or bank net banking passwords on our servers.
  • All transaction data is transmitted using industry-standard 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption meeting international PCI-DSS compliance standards.
  • Upon successful payment, Razorpay shares a verification reference (Payment ID), transaction status, and payer contact details so we can issue your tax/project invoice and commence work.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used strictly to:

  • Deliver customized web design, SEO, and brand positioning services as agreed.
  • Communicate project milestones, design mockups, and performance reports.
  • Issue booking confirmations, project receipts, and invoice documentation.
  • Send periodic growth guides or newsletters (only if voluntarily subscribed; you may unsubscribe at any time with a single click).
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and financial recordkeeping requirements.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personally identifiable information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with trusted operational infrastructure partners strictly necessary to provide our services:

  • Razorpay: To process secure UPI, card, and Net Banking payments.
  • ConvertKit / Kit: To manage optional newsletter communications.
  • Google Analytics: Anonymized metrics to evaluate website usage and improve site performance.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses standard browser cookies and analytics tags to understand how visitors interact with our pages, optimize loading speeds, and enhance navigation. You may choose to disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting core browsing capability.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or request deletion of your contact data from our active databases, subject to legal and accounting record-retention requirements.
