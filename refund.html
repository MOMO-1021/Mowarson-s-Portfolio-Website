Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Mowarson Developer (mowarsondeveloper.netlify.app)

At Mowarson Developer, we believe in complete operational transparency, client trust, and delivering exceptional value. We maintain a fair, clearly documented cancellation and refund policy for all brand positioning, messaging strategy, web design, and marketing services.

Key Summary: You can cancel any booking within 24 hours of payment for a 100% full refund before project work has commenced. Milestone-based refunds are available during design stages, and all refunds are credited back to your original payment method via Razorpay within 5 to 7 business days.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • Initial 24-Hour Window: If you change your mind within 24 hours of completing payment and project discovery/strategy work has not yet started, you may request an immediate, no-questions-asked cancellation for a 100% full refund.
  • Mutual Cancellation: If unforeseen scheduling conflicts or scope mismatches arise prior to kickoff, either party may agree to cancel the engagement amicably with a full refund of any unearned fees.

2. Milestone-Based Refund Schedule

Because bespoke web development and marketing strategy require substantial creative and technical effort upfront, refunds requested after kickoff are handled based on project milestones:

  • Before Initial Design Preview / Mockup Approval: If you are unsatisfied with the initial creative direction, an 80% refund of the package fee will be issued (20% retained to cover initial research, competitor analysis, and planning).
  • During Code Development / Staging: If cancellation occurs after the design concept is approved and custom front-end development is underway, a 50% refund will be issued.
  • Post-Delivery & Launch: Once the final website code is handed over, live domain deployed, or marketing campaigns launched, fees are non-refundable as the digital deliverable has been fully provided.

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

  • All approved refunds are initiated through our Razorpay Payment Gateway account directly to the original source method (UPI, Bank Account, or Credit/Debit Card).
  • In accordance with standard banking and Razorpay settlement cycles, funds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Mowarson Developer does not charge any administrative cancellation penalty.

4. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To request a cancellation or refund, please submit your request along with your Razorpay Payment ID:

  • Email: Send an email to mowarson5434@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request - [Your Name / Payment ID]".
  • WhatsApp: Message us directly at +91 6009825938 with your payment screenshot or reference ID.

We review and respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours.
